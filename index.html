ColdTrail

Solgram's promise is that food arrives as cold as it left the store. The weak point is the handover: the minutes between the walk-in and the rider, then the road. This board shows where it broke on one evening shift, what it cost, and which cause to fix first.

Limits used. Chilled at or below 5 °C and frozen at or below −18 °C, the figures in FSSAI's Schedule 4 hygiene requirements for storage and transport. What is real here. The tracker, the excursion rules, the hold policy and the API shapes all run in this page. The sensor readings are generated: 40 orders with fixed seeds, most clean and a few with faults. Item prices are placeholders. Next steps on real orders.
